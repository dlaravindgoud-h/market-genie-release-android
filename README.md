# Market Genie - Indian Stock Market Paper Trading App 📈

Practice trading Indian stocks (NSE) with realistic virtual capital and authentic tax calculations — 100% risk-free.

---

## 🌟 What is Market Genie?

**Market Genie** is a full-featured paper trading app designed for stock market enthusiasts, active traders, and beginners who want to learn and master Indian equity trading without putting real money at risk.

You start with **₹10,00,000** in virtual trading capital to simulate real market conditions, test strategies, and build confidence before entering live markets.

---

## ✨ What You Can Do With It

- **Trade Popular NSE Stocks Risk-Free**: Search and track prominent Indian companies (Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank, Tata Motors, Infosys, and more) with real-time price updates.
- **Realistic Statutory Taxes & Charges**: Understand the real impact of trading costs on your returns. Market Genie automatically calculates:
  - Brokerage
  - Securities Transaction Tax (STT)
  - Exchange Transaction Charges
  - GST
  - SEBI Turnover Fees
  - Stamp Duty
  - Depository Participant (DP) exit charges
- **Know Your Exact Breakeven Target**: See the minimum target price needed to cover all entry and exit taxes with zero net loss before placing your trade.
- **Interactive Technical Price Charts**:
  - Candlestick and line chart views.
  - **Pinch to zoom in/out** and swipe horizontally across historical price action.
  - Timeframe selection (1 Day, 5 Days, 1 Month, 6 Months, 1 Year, 5 Years).
  - Clear date markers on the horizontal axis showing days of the month.
  - Overlay indicators: **89 Moving Average** and **Supertrend** trend-reversal markers (▲ Buy / ▼ Sell).
  - Tap any candle to inspect Open, High, Low, Close, and indicator values.
- **Comprehensive Portfolio Tracking**: Monitor your open positions, unrealized and realized profit/loss, and itemized taxes paid on closed trades.
- **Easy Portfolio Reset**: Want to try a brand new trading experiment? Reset your virtual portfolio back to ₹10,00,000 anytime with a single tap.

## 📱 How to Download & Install on Android

Follow these simple steps to install Market Genie on your Android phone or tablet:

### Step 1: Download the APK
1. In this repository, click on the **[`market-genie.apk`](market-genie.apk)** file.
2. Click the **Download** (or **View Raw**) button to save the file to your device.

### Step 2: Open the Downloaded File
- Once the download finishes, swipe down your notification shade and tap the download completion notification, or open your phone's **Files** / **Downloads** app and tap **`market-genie.apk`**.

### Step 3: Allow Installation from Your Browser / Files App (If Prompted)
- If your phone displays a security prompt such as *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
  1. Tap **Settings** on the prompt.
  2. Toggle on **"Allow from this source"**.
  3. Tap the back button to return to the installer.

### Step 4: Complete Installation
- Tap **Install**.
- Once installed, tap **Open** to launch **Market Genie** and start paper trading!
